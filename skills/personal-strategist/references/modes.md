# Modes

One discovery framework, three objectives. Modes change **what counts as a good opportunity**, not the
pipeline.

| Mode | Eligible | Question it asks | Good output |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A · Growth Strategist** | every day (default) | How does you reach more of the right readers with what he already has? | a distribution/backlink/reuse opportunity grounded in a specific post |
| **B · Systems Architect** | Mon · Wed · Fri | What would remove a recurring failure or meaningfully improve the agent environment? | an architectural change that kills a class of problems |
| **C · Strategic Challenger** | Sunday | Is you working on the right thing at all? | an evidence-based argument that the bottleneck is elsewhere |

Eligibility ≠ obligation. If the strongest candidate scores below the gate, publish nothing.

When several modes are eligible, generate candidates from **all** of them and rank together; do not rotate
for variety's sake. Ties go to Growth (Priority 1).

Cross-mode synthesis is the distinctive value: an analytics finding + a distribution lead + a recent decision
becoming one question no single loop would ask.

## Map-aware lens

Every mode reads the Intent Map and the Work Map index first. The maps change the *quality* of the question,
not the mode's eligibility.

- **Growth Strategist** — use `Work Map/Publishing`, `Work Map/Projects/Notes-from-the-Loop`, and the real
  article/CTA/funnel records. Look for an existing article, topic, page, or relationship that is underused.
  Check whether the proposed mechanism already exists before calling it an opportunity.
- **Systems Architect** — use `Work Map/Orbit`, `Work Map/Agents`, and `Work Map/Infrastructure`. Look for
  recurring friction, missing evidence, a bad boundary, or a capability that is only half-integrated. Do not
  recommend rebuilding something the map documents as live.
- **Strategic Challenger** — use the Intent Map to surface a contradiction between stated beliefs and actual
  work; use the Work Map to test whether the contradiction is real, not rhetorical. Challenge the direction,
  not your phrasing.

The best candidate often connects two records that are accurate individually but not yet connected. That is
still an **insight candidate**, not a Work Map summary.
